Redlinr

Brand strategy — AI-powered contract comparison for B2B SaaS sales teams

Brand positioning

For Sales Directors and VP Sales at B2B SaaS companies who lose deals because contract negotiations drag on for weeks, Redlinr instantly surfaces what changed, what's risky, and what needs your attention — so your team closes faster, without waiting on legal.

Messaging pillars

Pain: "I don't know what changed in the new version they sent."

See every change in seconds, not hours

Redlinr compares contract versions instantly, highlighting every addition, deletion, and substitution — no manual side-by-side reading required.

Pain: "Legal takes 5 days to review a 3-page amendment."

Stop letting legal be your bottleneck

Flag the clauses that actually need attorney eyes. Skip the ones that don't. Your legal team reviews 20% of the document, not 100%.

Pain: "My rep agreed to a term we never agreed to before."

Know before you sign, not after

Redlinr surfaces risky language — liability shifts, auto-renewal traps, penalty clauses — before your rep pushes the contract across the table.

Pain: "We've done this deal three times. Why does it take the same amount of time?"

Turn repeat deals into a repeatable process

Benchmark incoming contracts against your standard terms. Know immediately how far off they are and what's non-negotiable for your team.

Brand voice & tone

Sound like this
  • Direct and specific
  • Confident, not arrogant
  • Outcome-first sentences
  • Plain English, no legalese
  • Respects the reader's time
Never sound like this
  • Revolutionary / game-changing
  • AI-powered everything
  • Legal-adjacent jargon
  • Vague benefits ("streamline")
  • Hedge language / passive voice

One-line pitch — cold outreach

"Most sales teams lose 10–14 days per deal waiting on contract reviews. Redlinr flags every change and risk in a contract the moment it arrives — so your reps know exactly what to push back on, and your legal team only touches what actually needs them."

Value proposition

Redlinr cuts the time between "they sent a new version" and "we know what to do next" from days to under a minute. It shows sales teams precisely what changed between contract versions, flags clauses that carry risk, and benchmarks incoming terms against your standard deal structure — so reps negotiate with confidence and legal reviews only what matters.

Differentiation — why this is not "another AI legal tool"

Built for sales, not legal

The interface, language, and alerts are designed for reps and sales leaders — not paralegals. No training required.

Comparison, not generation

Redlinr doesn't write contracts or suggest boilerplate. It tells you exactly what changed between two real documents.

Risk in context

Flags risky clauses with plain-English explanations tied to deal impact — not generic legal warnings.

Speed as the product

The core promise isn't accuracy or compliance — it's time. Every feature is built to remove wait time from the deal cycle.
