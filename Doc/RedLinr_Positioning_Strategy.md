## 4. WHAT WE ARE NOT (Critical Competitive Positioning)

### The Lines We Draw

#### ❌ NOT a Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) Platform
- We don't manage the full contract lifecycle (creation → negotiation → execution → renewal)
- We don't have legal playbooks, clause libraries, or approval workflows
- We don't replace Icertis, DocuSign, or Ironclad
- We complement them (can integrate via API)

**Why this positioning matters**: The biggest incumbent advantage is feature breadth. We win by being laser-focused on one problem. Claiming to be a "CLM" puts us in a feature race we will lose. Being a "comparison specialist" puts us in a speed race we will win.

#### ❌ NOT a Legal/Compliance Tool
- We're not intended to replace legal review
- We flag changes; humans make decisions
- We require review by someone with authority (not solo automation)
- Use case: "Heads up—here's what changed. You review and decide."

**Why this matters**: Legal risk is real; we own it by setting clear expectations. Courts don't liability-shift to software. We're a "second set of eyes," not a substitute for judgment.

#### ❌ NOT a General Document Comparison Tool
- Not a Word diff visualizer (that's a feature, not our product)
- Not a generic "contracts + PDFs" tool with AI tacked on
- We're specialized for contracts (understand key terms, pricing structure, commercial implications)

**Why this matters**: Differentiation from Document360, ShareFile, or PDF comparison tools. We know contracts.

#### ❌ NOT for Complex Legal Negotiations
- Best for straightforward redlines (pricing, payment terms, dates, liability caps)
- Not ideal for nuanced policy/jurisdiction changes
- Not a substitute for experienced contract counsel on M&A, complex IP, regulatory

**Why this matters**: Clear ICP boundary. We're for procurement/sales, not on-the-stand litigation. Manages expectations.

### Competitive Positioning: The 2x2

|  | **Specialized** | **General** |
|---|---|---|
| **Full CLM** | ❌ Not us (Agiloft, Ironclad on one platform for one vertical) | Icertis, DocuSign CLM, Conga (what incumbents do) |
| **Comparison-Only** | ✅ **RedLinr** (fastest, easiest, most accurate for diff) | Word's Track Changes, ShareFile, basic PDF tools |

**RedLinr's quadrant**: Specialized comparison, not full CLM. That's our moat.

## 8. BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Core Brand Voice
**Clear. Fast. Specialist.**

- **Clear**: Plain English, no jargon. "Pricing changed from $100K to $120K" not "financial obligation variance detected"
- **Fast**: Action-oriented language. Verbs first. "Spot." "Catch." "Close." Not "enable," "empower," "drive"
- **Specialist**: Confident in narrow scope. "We compare contracts. That's it." Not trying to be everything for everyone.

### Tone Rules

**✅ DO:**
- Use numbers (60 seconds, 1,500×, 70%)
- Lead with pain/outcome, then solution
- Address specific ICPs by name (Sales Director, Procurement Manager)
- Contrast vs. incumbents (not by bashing, but by clarifying: "We do comparison; they do full CLM")

**❌ DON'T:**
- Use generic SaaS language ("empower," "drive value," "synergize," "best-in-class")
- Claim we're a CLM replacement
- Overpromise (never: "eliminates legal review," "catches 100%")
- Use legal terminology if there's a simpler way
